Parking 101
Free parking in NYC is doable. Here's how.
✕
The money math
Manhattan garages run $500–$1,000+ a month. The street costs $0 — the whole trick is moving your car about twice a week for cleaning, and a missed window is a ~$65 ticket. Learn the rhythm once and that's roughly $4,800–$12,000 a year back in your pocket.
