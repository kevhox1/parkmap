## Findings

### 🔴 Blocking
None.

### 🟡 Significant

**#1 — Viewport-rung "ANY segment clears" resolution defeats §0.8.10's own stated guardrail in the realistic common case, not just a rare edge case.**
- Where: `resolveParkAndForgetViewport` (`ContentView.swift:5766`-ish), consumed by the goal-recompute block in `rebuildOverlays`.
- What: the global target is "the first (widest) rung at which **at least one** loaded segment clears," walked top-down. `steppedDown` (which drives the "Nothing clear for a full week — showing the best available" banner) is only `true` when the picked rung is not the widest. The engineer's own test (`testResolveParkAndForgetViewport_collectivelyClears_oneGoodSegmentAmongBadOnes`) proves this explicitly: one metered-only/unregulated segment among many `.noParking` segments resolves the *entire viewport* to the 8-day rung with `steppedDown: false` — **no banner at all**, even though every other segment in view will render red against an 8-day bar it can't possibly clear.
- Why this matters more than it looks: Kevin's own ruling data (cited in the spec's amendment banner) says **9.2% of parkable curb has no weekly move-forcing rule at all** (metered-only/unregulated — clears any window once meters are ignored). In a viewport with even a few dozen loaded segments, the probability that *zero* of them are in that 9.2% bucket is close to zero — meaning in almost every real Manhattan viewport, the top rung will be "cleared by someone," the banner will almost never fire, and the map will routinely show one or two green dots surrounded by red with zero honesty-banner, which is close to the exact "reads as broken" failure mode §0.8.10 calls "the single biggest NEW risk this amendment introduces" and "not optional polish, it's the guardrail."
- Is this an AC violation? No — AC-29's literal text (zero matches at the bottom rung) is satisfied. But the prose intent of §0.8.10 ("when the ladder has to step down for most/all visible segments, the UI must say so") is not met by an "ANY segment anywhere" test.
- Repro: load a viewport containing one metered-only or unregulated segment plus several ASP-regulated segments that only clear 1-3 days; select Park & Forget — expect mostly-red map, no banner.
- Recommend: change the trigger to a density/ratio check (e.g., "top rung chosen only if some minimum fraction of loaded segments clear it," or simplest: compare match-count at the picked rung vs. at the bottom rung and step down the *banner* if the top-rung match-count is below some small threshold) rather than a boolean "did anyone clear it." This doesn't need to change the color bucket, only the banner's own trigger condition.
- Owner: `@ios-engineer`.
