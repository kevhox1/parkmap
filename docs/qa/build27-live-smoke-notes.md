## Screenshot evidence (Tue 2026-11-24, 1:15–1:21 pm, NoHo/East Village) — `assets/build27-smoke/NN.png`

- **01–03 goal tiles (finding 7):** at 1:15 pm Overnight (01) and Park & Forget (03) render almost IDENTICAL, mostly green. A Few Hours (02) is almost ALL red: Broadway, W 3rd, W 4th, Mercer. Hypothesis: "A Few Hours" ("No meter, no cost") treats daytime metered/commercial curbs as not-free, while Overnight and P&F don't evaluate the current daytime window (or ignore meters). Either the semantics differ silently, or the windows are wrong. Park & Forget (multi-day) also can't plausibly equal Overnight on ASP streets. Audit each goal's [start, end] window and which categories count as free.
- **04 Bleecker St, drive, Overnight:** Left "—", Right "No parking". The map shows red on both curbs.
- **05 Bowery at Bond/E 2nd (findings 8/9):** the map draws a long GREEN line on Bowery's west curb, but the card says Left "—" (no data) and Right "No parking". So the green map segment and the card's left side don't even agree on whether data exists. That's the Bowery data bug PLUS a map/card segment mismatch.
- **06 → 07/08 E 2nd St (finding 12):** same street, same text ("Free until Monday 9:00 AM" / "Free until Friday 9:00 AM"). At 1:18 both chips are GREEN, at 1:19 both are RED, and the map lines stay green throughout. Under Overnight ("Good through Thu") both texts clear the window, so green is right. The red is a chip-color override bug (`leftChipColorOverride`/`rightChipColorOverride`), probably evaluated against a different segment or window than the text and the map.
- **09 E 1st St:** chips RED ("Free until Friday 9 AM" / "Free until Monday 9 AM") while the street lines are green. Same bug as 07/08. The parked-car icon IS visible here (top, E 1st St).
- **10 browse after moving the car (finding 13):** no car icon visible, but a horizontal DASHED BLUE LINE crosses the screen at the user dot. Check whether that's a stale car-pin/route artifact and why the car annotation dropped after re-placement.
- **Also odd:** the Overnight chip reads "Good through Thu" on a Tuesday afternoon. Check the goal-summary copy logic (overnight should read like "Good until tomorrow 8 AM", or explain the Thu).
