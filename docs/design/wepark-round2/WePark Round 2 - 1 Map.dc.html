Covers A1 (Overnight end-time lever), A2 (app icon and logo), A3 (icon map) and B2 (map states). Built from design system v0.1. Clock is Wed 10:11 PM in every screen. Words inside round buttons ("More", "Locate") stand in for SF Symbols; the symbol names are listed under each screen.

A1 · Overnight end-time lever

Proposal: the end time in the headline is the thing you change. A "Change" button sits beside it only while Overnight is selected. Tapping it swaps the line under the headline for five hour chips; picking one closes the row and updates the headline, the Overnight tile and the map. New part: hour chip row.

A2 · App icon and logo

Sketches in plain shapes, to pick a direction. The car inside the token is a placeholder block; the final glyph should be drawn properly at 1024 × 1024 before shipping. Recommended: Token on the curb.

The yellow car token sits on a solid green curb that turns into a broken coral one: the product's one idea in one picture. Ink-violet field so it stands apart from the many blue and green parking icons. In tinted mode the solid and broken lines still tell park from don't without colour.
