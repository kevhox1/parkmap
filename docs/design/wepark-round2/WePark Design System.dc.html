WEPARK DESIGN SYSTEM · V0.1

Stoop, straight

The rules, colours, type and parts every WePark screen is built from. Based on option 2a in WePark Directions. Press the raised pieces on this page; they behave as they will in the app.

RULE 1
Facts are flat

Verdicts, times, addresses and sign text sit on flat surfaces in plain words.

RULE 2
Raised means press or earned

A hard shadow under a piece means you can push it down, or you earned it. Nothing else is raised.

PARTS ADDED IN ROUND 2 (v0.2)
Map toolbar. Search field, More, Locate above the sheet. Flat, 1.5 line, 48 tall. B2
End-time lever. "Change" beside the headline while Overnight is chosen; opens hour chips, 52 tall. A1
Map legend card. Flat, five line patterns. B2.3
Verdict block. Park: green fill. Soon: amber fill. Don't: dashed coral outline. Metered: dotted blue outline. No data: grey outline. B4
Full-screen scaffold. Wordmark, close pill, headline 32/600, body 17, rows, one button at the bottom. Used for Spot Pass, the App Clip, onboarding and blocking errors. B10
Choice chip. White, 2 line, radius 20, 56 to 64 tall. The chosen one is taxi and raised 4. B10.1
List card. White, 1.5 line, radius 20, rows 56, hairline #E4E3EC. A row ends in a chevron, a switch or a value. B15
Switch. 56 × 34, 2.5 line; on is green, off is #E4E3EC. B15.2
Limit row. Dashed coral outline with the fix button on the right, placed next to the feature it limits. B18.2
Coach card and ring. Flat card, 2.5 line, plus a 4pt taxi ring around the target. No scrim. B1.6
Trip frame and tag. 4pt dashed #6A4CFF frame around the screen and a striped violet tag with the dates. B14.4
Stamp and hollow token. "SPOT PASSED" dashed stamp for a finished pass; a hollow dashed circle for "nothing here". B10.7
Drive side bubbles. All four states (park, don't, no data, checking) and their sizes. B5
Live Activity and Dynamic Island. Five lock-screen states and three island sizes. B12
