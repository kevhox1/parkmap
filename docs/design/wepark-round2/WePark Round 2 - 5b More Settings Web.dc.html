B16 · Parking 101

One scrolling page, shown here in three parts. Each card is a sign in one or two sentences, then the line WePark draws for it, so the lesson and the map use the same marks. Please check the rule wording against your own copy before it ships.

B16.1 · Top of the page
Parking 101
Close
Street cleaning
The sign with a broom. No parking on the posted days and hours so the sweeper can pass. It is suspended on some holidays.
Free outside those hours
No parking during them
No stopping, no standing, no parking
No stopping: don't stop at all, not even for a moment.
No standing: you can stop only to let people in or out.
No parking: you can stop to load or unload, but you can't leave the car.
All three: No parking
The three "no" signs share a card because the useful thing is how they differ. For leaving a car they all mean the same, and the card says so.
