B5 · Drive mode

Each side has four possible states, and each has its own outline so color is never the only cue: solid green fill (park), dashed coral outline (no parking), thin grey outline (no sign data), dotted violet outline (still checking). The parkable side is always the larger one. Voice reads the bubble words as written.
