THE RULE
Raised with a hard shadow means you can press it or you earned it: the car token, the chosen goal, Park here, karma, Pass your spot. Everything flat is a fact.
WORDING
"Yes!" and "Nope" become "Park" and "No parking". "You're good for 4d 11h" becomes "Free to stay until Mon 9:00 AM". "Nudge me" becomes "Remind me".
MAP
Curbs lose the candy dots: solid green to park, a quieter dashed line for don't. Map chips drop the heavy outline. The window's end time is now the headline of the bottom sheet.
