# Revenue concepts brainstorm

**Status:** Brainstorm, 2026-11-27. Nothing here is scheduled. These are unverified ideas. Partner terms, money estimates and legality are being checked by `@partnerships`, `@finance-fundraising` and `@legal-compliance`; see their docs under `docs/company/`.
**Existing plan:** WePark Pro, $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr, gating reminders and automation (`docs/business-model.md`).

**WePark's unfair advantages** (every idea should use at least one):
1. We know **where the car is**, down to the curb.
2. We know **when trouble is coming**: street cleaning, snow, meters, a long stay, a tow-away zone.
3. We know **what the curb rules are**, block by block, with "unclear is red" honesty.
4. We have **neighbors on the street** (community pins, Spot Pass).
5. Plan a Trip knows **where drivers are headed and when**.

## A. Consumer subscriptions and passes

| # | Idea | How it earns | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **WePark Pro** (current plan) | $4.99/mo, $29.99/yr | Reminders, Drive mode, Plan a Trip. The baseline. |
| A2 | **Ticket Guarantee** ("park on green, we cover the ticket") | Bundled into Pro or a higher tier at about $7.99 | Huge marketing hook: it shows we trust our own data. The "unclear is red" rule is what makes it affordable. Cap per year, require the car pin was on green at the time. **Could count as insurance, so legal check needed.** |
| A3 | **Visitor Day Pass** | $1.99–$2.99 one-time for 24–72h | For people driving into Manhattan for a show, game or visit who'll never subscribe. Ties to Plan a Trip. |
| A4 | **Household plan** | $7.99/mo for 2–4 cars or drivers | Shared car, both partners get reminders, Spot Pass between them. |
| A5 | **Employer / building perk** | Companies, hospitals or building managers buy seats | Nurses, teachers and doormen street-park daily. A hospital buying 200 seats is one sale. |

## B. Transactions and marketplace

| # | Idea | How it earns | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Car services** (snow dig-out, curbside wash, jump start) | Referral fee, or a 20–25% markup | See `car-services-marketplace-concept.md`. Shown at the moment of need. |
| B2 | **Street-cleaning car mover** ("car sitter") | Monthly concierge fee or per move, WePark takes a cut | Already an informal NYC market. Biggest liability: keys plus a licensed driver. |
| B3 | **Private spot rentals** (driveways, church and school lots, building spots) | Take a cut of each booking | JustPark/Airbnb model. Hosts list idle spots; WePark shows them when the street has nothing green. |
| B4 | **Spot Pass thank-you tips** | Optional $1–3 tip to the neighbor who handed off, WePark keeps a small fee | Tip, never a price. **Selling public street spots got apps banned elsewhere (MonkeyParking, SF 2014). Legal check needed.** |
| B5 | **Ticket help** | A share of the fines we help cut, or a referral to an existing ticket-fighting service | We hold evidence: the sign data, the car pin's time, the rule we showed. "Contest this ticket" button. |
| B6 | **Tow recovery helper** | Flat fee or referral | "Your car isn't where you left it": check NYC tow records, give directions to the pound, list the paperwork. A panic moment where people pay. |

## C. Referrals and affiliates (low effort, money per action)

| # | Idea | How it earns | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Garage fallback** | Affiliate commission from SpotHero / ParkWhiz / garages | When Plan a Trip or the map shows no green near the destination: "No street parking at 7 PM. Garage 1 block away, $22." Natural, honest, high intent. |
| C2 | **Auto insurance quotes** | Cost per lead | Auto insurance leads pay among the highest rates in affiliate marketing. Shown in My Car or settings, never pushy. |
| C3 | **EV charging and curbside chargers** | Affiliate or sponsor | NYC is adding curbside chargers. Show them for EV owners. |
| C4 | **Car upkeep reminders** (registration, inspection, oil change) | Referrals to mechanics and inspection stations | Inspection and registration lapse tickets are real. We already do reminders well. |
| C5 | **Movers and moving-day parking** | Referral to moving companies | "Moving? Here's the curb plan for the truck." Moving leads are valuable. |
| C6 | **Car-share and rental** | Affiliate | For users who give up on owning a car: Zipcar, Getaround. Odd fit, but real money. |

## D. Local sponsorship (careful, never creepy)

| # | Idea | How it earns | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Neighbor rewards funded by local businesses** | Businesses pay to give rewards to people who report | From `nyc-neighbors-incentives-concept.md`. A coffee for reporting a sweeper. |
| D2 | **Sponsored pins** (car washes, garages, mechanics) | Monthly listing fee | Clearly labeled "Sponsored". Never shown as a parking spot or in green. |
| D3 | **"Parked near" offers** | Cost per redemption | After you park: "You're 2 blocks from X, 10% off." Opt-in only, and the location never leaves the phone. |

## E. Business customers (B2B)

| # | Idea | How it earns | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **WePark for Fleets** | $X per vehicle per month | Plumbers, contractors, home health, florists and delivery vans get NYC tickets constantly. The commercial rules (loading zones, commercial meters) are an extension of the data we have. Biggest dollar opportunity per customer. |
| E2 | **Curb data API** | License fee or usage pricing | Clean block-by-block rules plus "what's free when". Buyers: navigation apps, ride-hail, delivery routing, autonomous-vehicle companies. |
| E3 | **Parking score for apartment listings** | License to listing sites, or affiliate | "Street parking: easy / hard" on StreetEasy-style listings. Car owners care a lot when choosing an apartment. |
| E4 | **City, BID and community board analytics** | Contracts or grants | Curb use, sweeper timing, double-parking hot spots from community reports. Slow sales, but sticky. |
| E5 | **Insurance data partnership** | License | Where cars park overnight relates to damage and theft risk. **Privacy-heavy: only aggregated, only with opt-in.** |

## F. Wildcards

- **F1 Venue partnerships:** a Broadway theater, MSG or Yankee Stadium pays to put "Driving? Plan your parking" in ticket confirmation emails.
- **F2 Dealership bundle:** NYC car dealers give a year of Pro with each car sold.
- **F3 Branded merch / "Good Neighbor" status:** low money, high community value.
- **F4 Paid early access to new boroughs:** Brooklyn residents pre-pay to unlock their borough faster (crowdfunding the expansion).

## First take on ranking (to be checked by the agents)

**Do first (fits now, low risk):**
1. **A1 Pro + A3 Visitor Day Pass.** Simple purchases, on a plan we already have.
2. **C1 Garage fallback.** Tiny build, honest, money from day one, and it makes "no green here" useful instead of a dead end.
3. **A2 Ticket Guarantee as the marketing story**, if legal allows. Even a small capped version makes the brand.

**Big bets (more work, bigger money):**
4. **E1 WePark for Fleets.** The B2B money.
5. **B1 Car services**, starting with snow dig-out.
6. **E2 Curb data API**, once the data is trusted.

**Park unless something changes:** B4 tips (legal risk), E5 insurance data (privacy), B2 car mover (liability).

## Combined ranking after the checks (2026-11-27)

Sources: `docs/company/partnerships/2026-11-27-revenue-partner-check.md`, `docs/company/finance/2026-11-27-revenue-sizing.md` (C1 corrected to the ~2.4% rate), `docs/company/legal/2026-11-27-revenue-concepts-risk.md`. All money figures are estimates; there's no usage data yet.

**Big lesson:** consumer money is small until ~100k drivers. At 10k drivers no single consumer idea clears ~$10k/yr. Business customers are where the dollars are.

| Rank | Idea | ~$/yr at 10k drivers | Legal | Next step |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A1 Pro** (+ A3 Visitor Pass) | ~$8k + ~$4k | Green | Already planned. Join Apple's Small Business Program (15% cut) before launch. |
| 2 | **E1 Fleets** | ~$11k at just 10 customers | Lawyer before contracts | **Interviews only, no build.** Existing products fix tickets after the fact; WePark would prevent them at the curb. If 3+ fleet owners say they'd pay, it beats all consumer lines combined. Needs commercial-rule data. |
| 3 | **C1 Garage fallback** | ~$1.7k | Green | Build for users, not money: it turns "no green here" into a next step. SpotHero ~2.4% via FlexOffers; ask SpotHero for direct terms. |
| 4 | **A2 Green Accuracy Promise** (renamed from Ticket Guarantee) | Small; break-even needs ~98.5% green accuracy | Lawyer needed (may count as insurance) | Marketing play. First **measure real green accuracy**; then a lawyer's written view. Structure: pays only when our data was wrong, capped, refund/credit, bundled in Pro, never says "insurance". |
| 5 | **B1 Snow dig-out** | ~$3k | Referral-only to a vetted, insured partner | Direct deal with one local operator beats TaskRabbit/Angi/Handy's ~5–10%. Seasonal. |
| 6 | **E2 Curb data API** | $40k–$200k at 2–10 customers | Lawyer before contracts; check NYC data licenses | Later, once the data has a measured accuracy record. |

**Free, user-friendly extras (no revenue, low risk):** B5 as a free "Contest this ticket" button that exports our evidence and links to the city's process; B6 tow helper as information only; C3 EV chargers on the map.

**Dropped:** B4 Spot Pass tips (SF banned spot-selling apps in 2014; a tip is barely different), B2 car mover, E5 insurance data (breaks "we don't sell your data"), F4 paid early access, C4 inspection/registration reminders (DMV already sends them free), C2 insurance leads (weak fit, consent rules).

**Needs a professional before money flows:** a lawyer for A2, fleet/API contracts, and service referrals; an accountant at entity setup and for sales tax on services.
